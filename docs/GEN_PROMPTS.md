# 角色立繪生圖指令（綠幕版）

適用：《老街迷香》6 位、《夜市無聲》7 位、《霧鎖山莊》7 位角色重畫。許晴已完成，不用再畫。

## 每次生成前先做這三件事
1. 把**許晴的新立繪**（四格那張）上傳當「畫風參考」。
2. 把**該角色目前的立繪**上傳當「長相參考」。
3. 貼上「共用指令」＋「該角色指令」，一次生一張 4 格圖。

格子順序固定（我會照這個順序切）：**左上、右上、左下、右下**。

---

## 共用指令（每個角色都要貼在最前面）

```
Use the first reference image as the ART STYLE reference (same line quality, painterly anime shading, warm skin tones, Studio-Ghibli-like realistic proportions).
Use the second reference image as the CHARACTER IDENTITY reference: keep exactly the same face, hairstyle, age, clothes and accessories.

Create ONE image, 1086x1448 (3:4 portrait), containing a 2x2 grid of four half-body portraits (head to waist) of the same character with four different expressions.

Layout rules:
- Each panel: character centered, slight 3/4 view, head near the top of the panel, body cut off at the waist.
- Leave a clear flat green gap between the four panels. Panels must not overlap or touch.
- Leave at least 8% empty green margin on the left and right of each panel. Shoulders and arms must NOT touch the left or right edge of the panel.

Background rules:
- Background is flat solid chroma-key green (#00FF00) only. No gradient, shadow, glow, texture, floor, or white outline.
- The only edge around the character is its own dark line art. No light or white fringe on hair strands; hair edges sharp and clean, not fuzzy.
- No green reflected on skin, hair or clothes.
- No text, no watermark, no panel borders.
```

## 重生成後怎麼交給我
- 檔名用「角色代號＋序號」，例如 `lan.png`、`jie.png`。
- 一個角色一張 4 格圖，直接傳就好；我會依左上、右上、左下、右下的順序切，換成遊戲裡對應的表情。
- 如果某個表情畫壞了，只重畫那一格也可以，但要單獨寫清楚是哪個角色、哪個表情。
